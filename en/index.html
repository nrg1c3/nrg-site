Badalak بَدَالَك

Pilot

“Not a translator. Badalak gets the errand done.”

A free web app for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people

Badalak is a free web app for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in Egypt. It opens in the phone’s browser, with nothing to install. It speaks for the user in clear Egyptian Arabic and shows the other person’s words as text with simple picture cards. It works at the pharmacy, the clinic, a branch counter and on WhatsApp, and every money or health decision goes back to the user.

Swipe to see more screens

  • Face to face: type or pick in pictures what you want, and Badalak says it aloud to the hearing person. Their words appear to you as text with cards (price, in stock or not, dose).
  • Read a photo: snap a prescription or any paper for Badalak to read.
  • Safety: checks your saved medicine allergies before any medicine order, and any money or health decision is shown to you as clear choices.
  • Medicine on WhatsApp: writes the medicine order for you to send from your own WhatsApp, and turns the pharmacy’s voice reply into text and cards.
  • “Wasl in place”: two QR stickers per branch — one for the customer, one for the branch phone. You pick your request in pictures, and the branch phone rings, vibrates and opens the chat. There is a “one phone” mode if the staff member has no phone.
  • Emergency: a siren and flashlight to alert people around you, sending your location to family, and ready phrases for ambulance and police.
  • Sound alerts: the phone vibrates for a doorbell, a crying baby or a fire alarm.
  • Picture mode: large icons and few words for people with limited reading.
  • Signs: teach Badalak your own signs, recognised on your phone. We are working on an Egyptian Sign Language library, still being filmed and reviewed.
  • “Call (pilot)”: Badalak calls on your behalf, and you read the call as text and answer from your phone. It is a pilot and not open to the public.
  • Opens in the phone browser, no download
  • Egyptian, Gulf and English
  • No account — your data stays on your phone
  • No ads, no selling data
  • The demo works offline after the first visit

Free for Deaf users: 30 tasks a month per phone; plans for businesses. We need 100 testers for the pilot.

For businesses: Egypt’s Law 10 of 2018 requires every company with 20 or more employees to employ at least 5% people with disabilities. Badalak helps Deaf employees work and be productive, and helps companies serve Deaf customers without an interpreter.

Sayvo سايفو

Testing with first users

“Sayvo: Say it once, it’s done” — a voice companion that organises your day

Speak, and Sayvo organises your tasks, appointments and bills and reminds you on time. It’s not a notes app — it follows up with you.

  • It follows up: reminds you before appointments, bills and medicine even when the app is closed, plans your day (what now, what next), breaks big tasks into small steps, and tells you how much you can spend until payday.
  • Sayvo Agent: “write an email to the client”, “WhatsApp Ahmed”, “book my doctor’s appointment” — it prepares them, and you approve.
  • Hyper Focus (in Sayvo Pro): focus sessions, “talk to Sayvo”, “whisper in a meeting”, and a “stressed” mode.
  • Home: a shared list for the household.
  • No account
  • Data stays on the phone
  • Numbers and emails are removed before speech leaves the phone
  • Free app + Pro subscription

Important: Sayvo is not a doctor or a treatment. It offers organisation and focus tools, not a diagnosis.
