Wasl وَصْل

Pilot

A free Arabic app for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people

Wasl speaks for you: you type what you want and it says it in a clear voice, and what the other person says appears to you as text with simple pictures. At the pharmacy, the doctor, the cashier, the bank, on transport and at government offices.

  • “Speak for me”: a smart assistant that finishes the task by talking to the hearing person, and comes back to you for any decision. It never agrees to anything without your choice.
  • Pharmacy on WhatsApp: order medicine, and voice messages turn into text cards (price, delivery time), with a warning if a substitute contains something you are allergic to.
  • “Wasl in place”: a QR code on your phone that staff scan, so you both chat in text on one screen.
  • Calls on your behalf, an emergency button, sound alerts, medicine reminders, and “picture mode” for people who find reading hard.
  • Works in the phone browser, no download
  • Egyptian, Gulf and English
  • Your data stays on your phone
  • No ads, no selling data

Free for Deaf users (30 tasks a month per phone); plans for businesses. We are now looking for 100 testers.

For businesses: Egypt’s Law 10 of 2018 requires every company with 20 or more employees to employ at least 5% people with disabilities. Wasl helps Deaf employees work and be productive, and helps companies serve Deaf customers without an interpreter.

Sayvo سايفو

Testing with first users

“Sayvo: Say it once, it’s done” — a voice companion that organises your day

Speak, and Sayvo organises your tasks, appointments and bills and reminds you on time. It’s not a notes app — it follows up with you.

  • It follows up: reminds you before appointments, bills and medicine even when the app is closed, plans your day (what now, what next), breaks big tasks into small steps, and tells you how much you can spend until payday.
  • Sayvo Agent: “write an email to the client”, “WhatsApp Ahmed”, “book my doctor’s appointment” — it prepares them, and you approve.
  • Hyper Focus (in Sayvo Pro): focus sessions, “talk to Sayvo”, “whisper in a meeting”, and a “stressed” mode.
  • Home: a shared list for the household.
  • No account
  • Data stays on the phone
  • Numbers and emails are removed before speech leaves the phone
  • Free app + Pro subscription

Important: Sayvo is not a doctor or a treatment. It offers organisation and focus tools, not a diagnosis.
